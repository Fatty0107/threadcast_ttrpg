# Melody Aleura — Overtone replacement

This text replaces **6. The Brake String — CTR** in the Melody Aleura affinity handout. All other Strings remain unchanged.

## Opening text to replace the handout's first two paragraphs

The air around Melody’s hands shimmers when she casts. She draws on a leyline and turns its energy into acceleration or force. She can perceive another caster’s active thread and push against it—or feed force into a willing caster’s intended output.

Counterpull opposes an active cast; Overtone strengthens a willing caster’s String. Neither copies another Affinity, controls the caster’s intent, sees the dormant leyline network, or makes a spell perform its opposite effect.

## 6. The Overtone String — CTR

**Input:** Melody’s own leyline draw and the perceptible, active thread of a willing caster.

**Conversion:** Feed calibrated force into that caster’s existing pull without redirecting it.

**Output:** A stronger Thread Check or one larger measurable result from that String.

**Timing:** Reaction as a willing caster within 30 ft begins or uses an ongoing String; Melody must perceive the active thread. Choose a benefit before its check or output resolves, and make her row’s Thread Check first. On failure, her TP is still added and the ally’s cast proceeds unboosted. A spent check cannot be boosted retroactively.

| PL | TP / DC | Effect — choose one before resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 / 11 | Add **+1** to the caster’s pending Thread Check; **or** add **1d4** to one eligible roll; **or** add up to **5 ft** to one eligible movement. |
| 2 | 2 / 13 | Add **+2** to the pending Thread Check; **or** add **1d6** to one eligible roll; **or** add up to **10 ft** to one eligible movement. |
| 3 | 4 / 15 | Add **+3** to the pending Thread Check; **or** add **2d6** to one eligible roll; **or** add up to **15 ft** to one eligible movement. |
| 4 | 6 / 17 | Add **+4** to the pending Thread Check; **or** add **3d6** to one eligible roll; **or** add up to **20 ft** to one eligible movement. |
| 5 | 9 / 19 | Add **+5** to the pending Thread Check; **or** add **4d6** to one eligible roll; **or** add up to **25 ft** to one eligible movement. |

**Boundaries:** An output bonus applies only if the caster’s own String succeeds. Add dice to **one target’s one damage, healing, or damage-prevention roll**, or add distance to **one creature’s movement produced by the String**, capped at that String’s printed movement distance. If no roll or distance qualifies, the caster can still take a pending-check bonus. Overtone adds no targets, area, duration, range, PL, save DC, conditions, or extra activations; it cannot change the String’s nature, waive its costs, or replace the caster’s concentration. Only one Overtone can benefit a cast or activation.

**Double edge:** Melody’s extra force increases only what the other caster has already shaped. If their String fails, stops, or has no eligible output, the power she committed does not become a new effect; her own Tension remains.
