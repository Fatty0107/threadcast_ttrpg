---
name: Player-scoped equipped dice
description: Why saved dice selection is account-wide rather than tied to an individual character.
---

**Rule:** Equipping a saved dice set applies it across that player's characters; saving a design alone does not change the equipped set.

**Why:** The user specifically asked for the die a player designs to become *that player's* rolling die once selected, while retaining each character's own Threadcast details and mechanics.

**How to apply:** When adding a new roll surface, resolve the selected design from the logged-in player's account rather than a character record. Keep the game result independent of the visual die.
