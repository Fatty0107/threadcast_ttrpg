---
name: Weavekeeper sheet grants
description: Ownership and mechanical boundaries for role-managed additions to character sheets.
---

**Rule:** Treat Weavekeeper additions as supplemental, role-owned entries in the player's normal sheet sections. Ordinary player edits and character-builder saves must not overwrite or mutate them. Skill attunement and expertise grants affect skill calculations; freeform feats, items, backgrounds, and notes remain descriptive until a rule for their mechanics is explicitly defined.

**Why:** The builder and ordinary sheet editor reconstruct or replace broad sheet snapshots, so mixing role-managed entries into player-editable arrays can silently remove grants or let players edit them. A supplemental background must not replace the mechanical creation background; a freeform feat or item description must not accidentally confer catalog bonuses.

**How to apply:** For new grant categories, preserve their separate ownership on every whole-sheet write, render them read-only beside the relevant player entries (including print), and add mechanical behavior only when explicitly specified.
