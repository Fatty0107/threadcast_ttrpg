---
name: Starting equipment edit safety
description: Why equipment packs behave differently for new and existing characters.
---

**Rule:** New characters receive their chosen background and guild equipment packs. Existing characters keep their current inventory when the builder is reopened; adding packs is an explicit opt-in, and changing background or guild must not silently remove previously acquired gear.

**Why:** A character may have consumed, traded, equipped, renamed, or received items after creation. Reconstructing inventory from the current background and guild would erase those player decisions or duplicate granted items.

**How to apply:** Treat pack provenance as descriptive ownership metadata, not a command to replace live inventory. Any future return/reconciliation flow for guild-issued gear should be explicit and preserve player changes.
