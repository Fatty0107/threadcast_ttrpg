---
name: Crown Standard legacy balances
description: Why existing characters were left without an inferred starting balance when the Crown Standard was introduced.
---

**Rule:** Do not retroactively assign background starting funds to existing characters that have no recorded currency. Let their balance remain unrecorded until explicitly entered; background grants apply to new character creation only.

**Why:** Before the Crown Standard, the character data had no implemented monetary balance or documented exchange rate. Assigning funds to pre-existing characters would invent an economic history, and applying the grant on later builder edits would reset earned or spent funds.

**How to apply:** In future economy work, distinguish an unrecorded balance from zero. Preserve recorded balances across sheet and builder saves. If an external deployment contains undocumented monetary data, inspect it before attempting any conversion.
