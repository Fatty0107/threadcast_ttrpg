So the equipment packs below would be a new website/game-system layer built around the existing material, rather than pretending those items are already specified in the rules.

Character Creation: Background & Guild Packs

I'd make character creation work like this:

Background → gives your upbringing, attribute bonuses, skills, and a Background Equipment Pack

Guild / Path → gives your professional affiliation, rank, guild benefits, and a Guild Equipment Pack

That means a player could end up with something like:

Background: Military Family
Guild: Scaled Guard

→ Military Family Pack
→ Scaled Guard Pack
→ Starting personal equipment
→ Starting weapons/armor selection

And the website automatically adds everything to their inventory.

5. IMPORTANT: DON'T JUST GIVE THEM THE ITEMS

I'd make every item added to the character have metadata:

Item:
Basic Warded Leather Gloves

Source:
Background — Military Family

Rarity:
Common

Quantity:
1

Bound:
No

Replaceable:
Yes

And:

Item:
Arming Sword

Source:
Guild — Scaled Guard

Rarity:
Common

Quantity:
1

Then later, if the player leaves the guild, the system can distinguish:

Personal equipment

from

Guild-issued equipment

That's going to matter a LOT once you start using guild ranks and obligations.

For example, a Scaled Guard character shouldn't necessarily get to leave the guild and casually keep every piece of guild-issued equipment forever.

6. I'd ALSO Add "Starting Gold / Funds"

Your inventory system already has rarity, availability, cost, and restrictions as part of its structure.

So instead of giving every character an arbitrary giant pile of money, I would eventually have:

Starting Funds

Background determines your personal starting funds.

Guild determines whether you have access to guild resources.

Then the player can spend their starting funds during character creation.

That gives you the D&D Beyond experience:

Starting Equipment

OR

Starting Funds
