# 5. The Fury String
**Domain:** Anger, indignation, and aggression.

Fury magnifies resentment or redirects anger toward a chosen focus. It can provoke reckless attacks, break disciplined formations, or drive an ally through pain.

**Combat role:** Taunting, forced aggression, offensive enhancement.

Its danger is tunnel vision. Someone affected by Fury may ignore allies, tactics, and self-preservation.

# 6. The Sorrow String
**Domain**: Grief, longing, and emotional heaviness.

Sorrow brings buried loss to the surface. It can weaken motivation, interrupt concentration, or make someone confront grief they have refused to acknowledge. Sorrow should not simply “make someone sad.” It finds an existing absence and gives it weight. Against someone with profound unresolved grief, it could be devastating.

**Combat role**: Debilitation, concentration disruption, reducing aggression.

# 7. The Rapture String
**Domain**: Joy, exhilaration, and euphoria.

Rapture intensifies pleasure and excitement. It can inspire allies, distract enemies, lessen pain temporarily, or overwhelm someone with euphoric sensory distortion.

**Combat role**: Buffing, distraction, temporary pain suppression.

Its dangerous expression is mania: confidence without judgment, energy without restraint, and happiness sharpened into recklessness.

# 8. The Shame String
**Domain**: Shame, guilt, and self-consciousness.

Shame forces a person’s awareness inward. It can make a liar falter, cause an attacker to hesitate, expose guilt, or trap someone in obsessive self-judgment.

**Combat role**: Hesitation, interrogation, disrupting social or magical confidence.

Shame cannot determine objective guilt. It works with what the target believes they should regret. An unrepentant person may have very little for Evander to grasp.

# 9. The Veil String
**Domain**: Emotional masking and concealment.

Veil hides emotional signals without removing the underlying feelings. Evander can make fear appear as calm, grief appear as indifference, or conceal a person’s emotional presence from supernatural perception. Unlike Hush, Veil does not numb the emotion. The target still feels everything beneath the mask.

**Combat role**: Infiltration, resisting emotional detection, preventing enemies from reading morale.

# 10. The Concord String
**Domain**: Emotional connection and synchronization.

Concord links the emotional states of multiple people. Allies can share courage, distribute panic so no single person is overwhelmed, or instinctively understand each other’s emotional intent.

It can also be used offensively: one frightened soldier might transmit panic throughout an entire unit. This would be especially powerful, and dangerous, in crowds.

**Combat role**: Group support, coordination, formation disruption.

# 11. The Devotion String
**Domain**: Existing bonds, loyalty, protectiveness, and attachment.

Devotion strengthens an authentic emotional bond already present. It can allow someone to endure pain for another, protect an ally instinctively, or resist effects that would make them betray someone they genuinely care about.

**Combat role**: Protection, loyalty, sacrifice, resistance to coercion.

This String must not create love. It can only strengthen a real bond. That restriction is essential because it gives Evander one kind of emotional magic he can trust: if Devotion answers, something genuine was already there.

At higher levels, he might sense that a bond exists without learning its exact nature.

# 12. The Catharsis String
**Domain**: Emotional release, acceptance, and transformation.

Catharsis allows accumulated emotion to be expressed and discharged safely. It can end magical panic, release suppressed grief, break emotional compulsions, or convert overwhelming feeling into a brief surge of clarity and power. Catharsis is not forgetting and does not remove meaningful grief. It lets a person feel something without being controlled by it.

**Combat role**: Cleansing emotional effects, recovery, emergency stabilization.
