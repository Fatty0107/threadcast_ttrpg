CONVERSION RULES
==================================================

10 Cinders = 1 Mark
10 Marks = 1 Crown
10 Crowns = 1 Sovereign

Therefore:

1 Cinder = 0.1 CR
1 Mark = 1 CR
1 Crown = 10 CR
1 Sovereign = 100 CR

CR should be treated as the canonical numerical accounting unit internally wherever practical.

For example:

37 CR can be displayed as:
3 Crowns + 7 Marks

or simply:
37 CR

450 CR can be displayed as:
4 Sovereigns + 5 Crowns

The system should NOT use floating-point arithmetic for currency if avoidable.

Do not create multiple competing representations of the same player's money.
