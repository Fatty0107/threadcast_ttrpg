# Aeolus — Luck Affinity Strings

*Player handout · proposed character-specific THREADCAST mechanics for Weavekeeper approval*

Aeolus learned Luck over cards in an Ovrashire tavern. She knows the difference between reading a table, playing well, and nudging a chance that was already there. Her Affinity favors **plausible outcomes**, not miracles on demand. The very slight breeze that passes her when she casts is her **magical signature**, not a Wind String.

This is a **catalogue of possible Strings**, not twelve Strings she begins with. Under the core rules a starting character has **two**; Aeolus and the Weavekeeper choose which she knows, and later unlocks follow normal progression. The numbers and effects below are a playable proposal, **not established canon**.

## Using this handout

- **PL** means Power Level. The **TP / DC** column uses the *core* PL costs and Thread Check DCs: **1 / 8, 3 / 11, 6 / 14, 10 / 17, 15 / 20**. A successful cast adds that Tension; failure and natural 1 use the core Mishap and Snapback rules. Above her Safe Limit she makes the normal Strain Check; exceeding her Thread Pool is an automatic Snapback. She can choose a lower PL than her maximum.
- **Check attribute:** Each String below shows **CTR**, the default basic Thread Check attribute. Add RB only if the relevant skill is Attuned. If Aeolus expresses a String through a specific Mode, use that Mode's printed check, proficiency, range, techniques, and Weaving rules instead; the label is **not** a permanent Mode assignment.
- On each cast choose **either** the *Favorable* or *Unfavorable* expression in that row, not both. Unless a row says otherwise, one cast affects one visible subject or one reachable mundane object within **30 ft**; a game or social scene requires a participant Aeolus can see or hear. Declare a roll-affecting cast **before the affected die is rolled**. A pending effect expires at the **start of Aeolus's next turn** in combat, or after **1 minute** outside combat, if not used first.
- A listed bonus or penalty changes **one specified nonmagical d20 check or attack roll** only. It cannot affect a Thread Check, Strain Check, saving throw, natural 1/20, already determined result, or a roll with no genuine uncertainty. No creature can benefit from, or suffer, more than **one Aeolus Luck modifier on the same roll**: use the strongest, never the sum. The same declared wager or event cannot be recast to farm attempts.
- Where an *Unfavorable* expression names a **RES save**, the target makes it against that row's DC **when the cast succeeds**. On a success, it avoids the penalty or condition; an effect that specifies half damage still deals half. Damage bonuses require a real hit by a real weapon or another existing source; Luck never creates an attack, an item, a person, a secret, or an impossible path.
- For a **damage-reduction or bonus-damage pool**, roll its listed dice **once when the effect is created**. Subtract the amount used from that total; any unspent amount vanishes when the effect ends. A single-hit reduction rolls its dice when that hit occurs, cannot reduce damage below zero, and cannot be saved for later hits.
- Unless stated otherwise, an ongoing effect requires **concentration** and ends if Aeolus begins concentrating on another String. The Weavekeeper adjudicates whether a plausible chance exists at all. A narrative opening grants only the precise benefit written here, not a guaranteed victory.

## 1. The First Hand String — CTR

**Purpose:** Aeolus's first awakening: a beginner drew one improbably useful hand. Favor an uncertain draw or unsettle someone else's read of it; she still has to know how to play. If a game has **no d20 check**, the Weavekeeper can instead describe a modestly favorable *plausible* draw for a successful cast; it never specifies a card or guarantees the game's result. This can happen **once per game**, not once per dealt card.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Gain **+1** on one check to play a game of chance she is actually taking part in. | A visible opponent takes **−1** on one check to read or bluff her in that game; **RES save** negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Gain **+2** on one game check involving a real draw, deal, or toss. | One opponent takes **−2** on one such game check; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Give **+2** to one willing participant's game check, including Aeolus's, while she observes the same table. | Up to **two** visible participants each take **−1** on their next game check; each gets a RES save. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Give **+3** to one participant's next game check in a contest with an uncertain outcome. | Up to **two** opponents each take **−2** on their next game check; each gets a RES save. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | Give **+3** to up to **three** willing players at the same table, for **one check each** during the next minute. | Up to **three** opponents at that table each take **−3** on **one check** during the next minute; each gets a RES save. |

**Double edge:** She can win a game with Luck; she cannot make winning prove she was the better player.

## 2. The Tell String — CTR

**Purpose:** Notice a tell without learning anyone's thoughts. This is useful in a tavern, an interrogation, or a fight.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | After watching a person for a minute, notice **one visible habit** relevant to their next action. | Point out that habit to one ally; they gain **+1** on one check to notice that person's next *physical* feint. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Gain **+2** on one check to identify a bluff or sleight of hand from someone she can observe. | One ally gains **+1** on their next attack against an opponent Aeolus watched act this round. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Identify which of **two visible choices** a watched person appears to favor; they can still change their mind. | Up to **two** allies each gain **+1** on their next attack against that watched opponent. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Gain **+3** on one check to detect a watched person's deception; this does not reveal the truth behind it. | One ally gains **+2** on their next attack against a watched opponent, before Aeolus's next turn. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | For **1 minute** of concentration, track the observable tells of up to **three** people in the scene; each of Aeolus's checks to read them gains **+2**. | Up to **three** allies each gain **+2** on **one attack** against watched opponents before her next turn. |

**Double edge:** She can read people brilliantly until they begin reading her.

## 3. The Nudge String — CTR

**Purpose:** Tip one small, genuinely uncertain action in someone's favor, without performing it for them.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Give herself **+1** on one nonmagical skill check. | One visible creature takes **−1** on its next nonmagical skill check; RES save negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Give herself or one willing ally **+2** on one nonmagical skill check. | One target takes **−2** on its next such check; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Give one willing ally **+2** on one attack roll or nonmagical skill check. | One target takes **−2** on its next attack roll or nonmagical skill check; RES save negates it. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Give one willing ally **+3** on one attack roll or nonmagical skill check. | One target takes **−3** on its next attack roll or nonmagical skill check; RES save negates it. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | Give **two** willing allies **+3** each on **one different roll** before her next turn. | Two visible targets each take **−3** on **one roll** before her next turn; each gets a RES save. |

**Double edge:** Nudging a choice is easy. Accepting that another person's success belongs to them is harder.

## 4. The Slip String — CTR

**Purpose:** Turn a direct blow into a glancing one. She redirects a chance of impact; she does not become untouchable.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Reduce the **next hit's damage** to herself by **1d6** before her next turn. | A visible attacker takes **−1** on its next attack against her; RES save negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Reduce one incoming hit to herself or an ally within **10 ft** by **2d6**. | One attacker takes **−2** on its next attack against a chosen ally within 10 ft; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Reduce one hit to an ally within **30 ft** by **3d6**. | Two visible attackers each take **−1** on their next attack against the protected ally; each gets a RES save. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Give a single ally within 30 ft a **4d6 damage-reduction pool** against hits for **3 rounds**, while Aeolus concentrates. | Two attackers each take **−2** on one attack against that ally during the next 3 rounds; each gets a RES save. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | Give up to **three** allies within 30 ft a **shared 6d8 reduction pool** for **3 rounds**, while concentrating; spend it only on actual hits. | Up to three attackers each take **−3** on their next attack against one of those allies during the next 3 rounds; each gets a RES save. |

**Double edge:** A narrow escape can make her feel invincible right up to the blow that does not miss.

## 5. The Opening String — CTR

**Purpose:** Make a vulnerable instant more likely to matter. The weapon and the attacker do the striking; Luck supplies neither.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | One ally's **next successful weapon hit** before her next turn gains **+1d6 damage**. | One visible opponent takes **−1** on its next check to keep its footing or hold a position; RES save negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | A single next successful weapon hit gains **+2d6 damage**. | A visible target takes **−2** on its next such positioning check; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | A single next successful weapon hit gains **+3d6 damage**. | A target takes **−2** on its next positioning check and loses **10 ft of movement** on its next turn on a failed RES save. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Divide a **4d6 bonus-damage pool** among up to **two successful weapon hits** before her next turn. | Two visible targets each lose **10 ft of movement** on their next turn on a failed RES save. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | Divide a **6d6 bonus-damage pool** among up to **three successful weapon hits** before her next turn. | Up to three targets each lose **15 ft of movement** on their next turn on a failed RES save. |

**Double edge:** The perfect opening tempts her to claim credit for risks someone else had to take.

## 6. The Misstep String — CTR

**Purpose:** Favor a harmless-looking error in movement: a poor footfall, a mistimed turn, an obstacle almost overlooked. No conjured banana peels.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Gain **+1** on one check to cross uneven, slippery, or cluttered ground. | One visible moving creature loses **5 ft** of its next turn's movement on a failed RES save. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Give one willing creature **+2** on such a traversal check. | One target loses **10 ft** of its next turn's movement on a failed RES save. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Give two willing creatures **+2 each** on one traversal check apiece. | One moving target loses **15 ft** of movement and falls **Prone** on a failed RES save, if the terrain makes a fall plausible. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Give up to three willing creatures **+3 each** on one traversal check apiece. | Two moving targets each lose **15 ft** and fall Prone on failed RES saves, where a fall is plausible. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | For **3 rounds**, give up to three willing creatures **+3 on one traversal check each** through the chosen hazardous area, while concentrating. | Up to three moving targets each lose **20 ft** and fall Prone on failed RES saves, only if their surroundings allow it. |

**Double edge:** She knows how to exploit a stumble; she might forget that someone else has to live with the fall.

## 7. The Loose Change String — CTR

**Purpose:** Be where a small, already-existing thing can be found or mishandled. This is the tavern scavenger's Luck, not material creation.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Gain **+1** on one search check for a mundane object known to be in the immediate area. | A target handling a **loose object** takes **−1** on its next check to keep hold of it; RES save negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Gain **+2** on one search check for a mundane object in the current room. | A target handling a loose object takes **−2** on its next check to hold it; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Give **+2** to up to two people on one search check each for a specific, present mundane object. | On a failed RES save, one target **drops one loosely held object** at its feet; not a secured or strapped item. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Gain **+3** on one check to find a mundane clue or object actually present in a searched area within **30 ft**. | On failed RES saves, up to two targets each drop **one loosely held object** at their feet. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | For **1 minute**, grant up to three searchers **+3 on one check each** to locate a specified, actually present mundane clue. | Up to three targets each drop one loosely held object on failed RES saves; no effect on secured gear. |

**Double edge:** A little thing found at the right time might also be a little thing someone else lost.

## 8. The Fork String — CTR

**Purpose:** Notice which of two real routes has better odds. She cannot cause a safe exit to exist where none does.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Get a general sense of which of **two visible routes** looks less physically hazardous right now. | An observer pursuing her takes **−1** on one check to follow her chosen route; RES save negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Gain **+2** on one check to navigate either of two routes she can inspect. | One pursuer takes **−2** on one check to track her route; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Guide up to two allies, each gaining **+2** on one check to traverse the chosen route. | Two pursuers each take **−2** on one check to track that route; each gets a RES save. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | For **1 minute**, guide up to three allies; each gains **+3 on one traversal check** along a real route she has observed. | Two pursuers each take **−3** on one tracking check along that route; each gets a RES save. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | For **3 rounds** of concentration, guide up to five allies; each gains **+3 on one traversal check** through a visible, physically passable route. | Up to three pursuers each take **−3** on one tracking check along that route; each gets a RES save. |

**Double edge:** Knowing the better road does not mean knowing where she wants it to take her.

## 9. The Wager String — CTR

**Purpose:** Accept a declared personal cost for a better chance. She must put something of her own on the table.

**Stake rule:** On a successful cast Aeolus loses the listed VP **immediately**, whether the later roll succeeds or fails; she cannot use Wager if this would reduce her below **1 VP**. The stake is ordinary VP loss, not transferable or refunded by the String. Declare the beneficiary and roll before casting. The *Unfavorable* expression still requires a real opponent and a RES save.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Stake **1 VP**; Aeolus gains **+2** on one nonmagical check. | Stake 1 VP; one opponent takes **−2** on one nonmagical check on a failed RES save. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Stake **2 VP**; she or an ally gains **+3** on one nonmagical check. | Stake 2 VP; one opponent takes **−3** on one nonmagical check on a failed RES save. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Stake **3 VP**; she or an ally gains **+4** on one attack roll or nonmagical check. | Stake 3 VP; one opponent takes **−4** on one attack roll or nonmagical check on a failed RES save. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Stake **5 VP**; one ally gains **+5** on one attack roll or nonmagical check. | Stake 5 VP; one opponent takes **−5** on one attack roll or nonmagical check on a failed RES save. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | Stake **8 VP**; two allies each gain **+6** on one different attack roll or nonmagical check. | Stake 8 VP; two opponents each take **−6** on one attack roll or nonmagical check on failed RES saves. |

**Double edge:** Betting on herself feels exhilarating until the cost comes due, win or lose.

## 10. The Second Chance String — CTR

**Purpose:** Give a *different* attempt a chance to succeed after a visible failed attempt. This never rerolls or changes the failure.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | After witnessing a failed nonmagical task, Aeolus gains **+1** on **her own new attempt** at it; the situation must still allow another try. | An opponent who just succeeded at a contested nonmagical task takes **−1** on a *new* attempt at the same task; RES save negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Aeolus or an ally gains **+2** on one permitted new attempt at a witnessed failed task. | One opponent takes **−2** on a permitted new attempt at a witnessed successful task; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Up to two allies each gain **+2** on one permitted new attempt after a witnessed failure. | Two opponents each take **−1** on one permitted new attempt after witnessed successes; each gets a RES save. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Up to two allies each gain **+3** on one permitted new attempt after a witnessed failure. | Two opponents each take **−2** on one permitted new attempt after witnessed successes; each gets a RES save. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | Up to three allies each gain **+3** on one permitted new attempt after a witnessed failure. | Up to three opponents each take **−3** on one permitted new attempt after witnessed successes; each gets a RES save. |

**Double edge:** Aeolus can make failure less final, but cannot spare anyone the memory of failing.

## 11. The Narrow Escape String — CTR

**Purpose:** Preserve someone from a *survivable* hazard. This bends a fall, a collapsing shelf, or a swing toward a less awful result; it does not undo death.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | Reduce the next instance of **environmental damage** to herself by **1d6** before her next turn. | A visible creature in a real environmental hazard takes **−1** on one check to avoid it; RES save negates it. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Reduce one environmental hit to herself or an ally within **10 ft** by **2d6**. | One creature in an existing hazard takes **−2** on one check to avoid it; RES save negates it. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Reduce one environmental hit to an ally within **30 ft** by **3d6**. | Two creatures in existing hazards each take **−2** on one avoidance check; each gets a RES save. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | Reduce one environmental hit to an ally within 30 ft by **5d6**; this cannot prevent an effect with no survivable outcome. | Two creatures in existing hazards each take **−3** on one avoidance check; each gets a RES save. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | For **3 rounds** of concentration, protect up to three allies within 30 ft with a **shared 6d8 environmental-damage reduction pool**. | Three creatures in real, immediate hazards each take **−3** on one avoidance check; each gets a RES save. |

**Double edge:** The person who walks away may mistake survival for proof that the risk was harmless.

## 12. The House Edge String — CTR

**Purpose:** Arrange several *small* chances across a room, as if Aeolus has finally learned to see the whole table. No one roll becomes certain.

| PL | TP / DC | Favorable expression | Unfavorable expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **1** | **1 / 8** | One willing ally within **15 ft** gains **+1** on one nonmagical check. | One opponent within 15 ft takes **−1** on one such check on a failed RES save. |
| **2** | **3 / 11** | Two allies within **20 ft** each gain **+1** on one different nonmagical check. | Two opponents within 20 ft each take **−1** on one such check on failed RES saves. |
| **3** | **6 / 14** | Three allies within **30 ft** each gain **+1** on one different attack roll or nonmagical check. | Three opponents within 30 ft each take **−1** on one different attack roll or nonmagical check on failed RES saves. |
| **4** | **10 / 17** | For **3 rounds** of concentration, three allies within 30 ft each gain **+2 on one attack or nonmagical check total**. | Three opponents within 30 ft each take **−2 on one attack or nonmagical check total** on failed RES saves. |
| **5** | **15 / 20** | For **3 rounds** of concentration, up to five allies within 30 ft each gain **+2 on one attack or nonmagical check total**. | Up to five opponents within 30 ft each take **−2 on one attack or nonmagical check total** on failed RES saves. |

**Double edge:** A crowded room can make everyone feel like part of her game. She still has to see them as people.

## What makes this Aeolus's Luck

Her first String can make a card game go her way, but years of practice explain why she can win fairly, too. **Tell** and **Fork** reward noticing people and possibilities; **Nudge** and **Wager** ask how far she is willing to tilt a contest; **Slip** and **Narrow Escape** turn combat luck into survival without erasing danger. At the table, the interesting question is not whether Aeolus can guarantee a win. It is **when she thinks someone deserves a fair chance**.
