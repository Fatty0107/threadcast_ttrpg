## Background

Aeolus was orphaned too young to remember how it happened. She grew up in Ovrashire, a city full of people passing through, and a tavern eventually became both her home and her window into other people's lives. She learned from its visitors, especially at the card table. Winning games—and sometimes wagers—became a way to pay for her room.

Her Luck awakened at seven, during her first lessons in cards. She lost the first game, did better in the second, and then won a round whose odds seemed to fall remarkably in her favor. She has practiced enough since then to be good at cards without relying solely on luck. How fair a game she plays can depend on how much she respects the person across the table.

The Thaumatarch appealed to her for its intelligence work. A connection to someone involved in that side of the guild offered a way in; the people, information, and questions were as compelling to her as the magic.

## Playing Aeolus

Aeolus is fun-loving and fascinated by human nature. She can find something to love about almost anything, and she wants to know the people around her—right up until someone starts asking equally searching questions about *her*. She is often charming, sometimes irritating, and capable of being mean.

Her ego is enormous and remarkably easy to bruise. She guards her own feelings so fiercely that she would rather deflect than open up, even when honesty would help her. Her sense of morality is not always straightforward; a choice that makes perfect sense to her may not look so simple to someone else.

**Fun fact:** She has anatidaephobia—the fear that somewhere, a duck is watching her.

**“Would you love me if I was—?”** She would not use the same question twice. She would tailor it to the person she is asking, choosing whatever she thinks would get the most interesting reaction.
